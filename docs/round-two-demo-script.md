# Round-two demo script (target 75–90 seconds)

Built from the judges' feedback in `docs/judging-notes-twist-e2338.md`. Jason's
rule for founder demos: **examples matter.** Every beat below uses a real,
substantive example on the live site, signed in with a real account, with no
seed data.

## Before recording

- Production has migrations 0015–0017 applied, seed content removed, and the
  1.9.0 extension installed.
- 10–15 real annotations exist from 2–3 real accounts, with real comments and
  follows, so the feed, trending rail, and "People worth following" are populated.
- One open request exists for the article you will annotate (from a second
  account), so beat 2 can answer it.
- Browser zoom 110%, side panel open, notifications off.

## Beats

| Time | On screen | Voiceover (say it in your own words) |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:08 | Feed, For You tab. Hover a **Dispute** badge, then a trending #topic. | "Annotated is the context layer of the web: highlight any sentence, dispute it or add what's missing, and everyone can see it." |
| 0:08–0:30 | A real news article or essay. The side panel shows **"On this page: 1 open request"**. Highlight **one specific sentence**, never the headline. Pick **Dispute** or **Fact check**. Type a sharp, specific take with one #topic. Click **Answer this request**. | Say what's wrong with the sentence in one line. |
| 0:30–0:38 | Click **Publish**. The permalink opens by itself. Show the comment first, the yellow-highlighted passage, the page screenshot, **File a claim** beside the clip, and the author's follower and annotation counts. | "No extra clicks: you land on the permalink, ready to share." |
| 0:38–0:55 | A TWiST or All-In episode on YouTube. Scrub the video and point out that **the clip follows the playhead**. Drag a handle; the video jumps with it. Pick **Context**, write the take, publish. Show the 240p clip playing on the permalink, bounded to the range. | "Up to 90 seconds, recorded at 240p, always linked to the original." |
| 0:55–1:05 | An X post. Annotate it as **Receipts** and tick **Also post my take to X**. The X composer opens with the take and link. Show the saved screenshot on the post. | "If the post gets deleted, the receipt survives." |
| 1:05–1:15 | **Requests** page: show answered and open requests. Click **Request an annotation**, ask a named expert to fact-check a story. | "Put a bounty of attention on a story: ask the person whose judgment you trust." |
| 1:15–1:25 | Feed: Following tab, a comment thread, **Invite by email**. End on the landing page with the Chrome Web Store button. | "Every claim on the web, with its context attached." |

## Don'ts (from the judges)

- Don't highlight a headline. Pick the paragraph or sentence that matters.
- Don't show placeholder metadata, seed personas, or test posts.
- Don't type timecodes; drag the handles.
- Don't make the viewer click "view post"; the permalink opens on its own.
